# Roadmap

Each phase is built on its own branch and reviewed on a real iPhone before the next one.
Nothing is merged into `main` until the product owner says so.

| Phase | Branch | Contents | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1a** | `phase-1a-foundation` | Xcode project (iOS 26), shared packages, `ConciergeKit` (Core + Data), SQLite schema v1, every setting, 11 languages with right-to-left, 4-tab shell with the branded top bar, launch animation, butler-with-clock icon (navy, brass→teal ring, bell badge), About pages listing all 4 apps, Offline AI screen and setup guide, JSON backup/restore, automatic backup to a chosen folder, CSV export, docs, unit tests | **In review** |
| **1b** | `phase-1b-alarms` | AlarmKit alarms (ring through silent mode and Focus, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island), alarm list and editor, repeat days, labels, one-off alarms, skip next, skip days off, default snooze 5 min + presets, wake log written by every ring, next-alarm widgets and the alarm Live Activity, **Calendars & Reminders: every account on the iPhone (phone, iCloud, Google, Outlook / Exchange / Microsoft 365, Yahoo) with per-calendar on/off and an "Add Google or Outlook" guide** | Built, awaiting device test |
| **2** | `phase-2-briefing` | Today's events and due reminders from every calendar account; importance by the offline AI (Gemma) where available, built-in rules otherwise; the script in 11 languages × 4 tones (title, short summary, start time, time until it starts, "looks important"), Quick / Standard / Full, private mode; iPhone voices chosen per accent, gender and quality; **Brief Me Now** with a live transcript; Today list with Important badges; **Preview Voice**, **Hear Every Voice** (all accents and tones), **Preview My Briefing**; guide to download Enhanced / Premium voices | **In review** |
| 2b | `phase-2b-voices` | **Built-in offline voices, bundled inside the app** (product owner, Nov 2026: no download, no setup on the iPhone): sherpa-onnx ≥ 2.0 (Apache-2.0, no GPL espeak-ng) with Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) for American and British English male and female, and MeloTTS English (MIT) for Indian English; Apple's voices for the other gender of Indian English and for languages without a licence-safe voice. Expected app size increase about 330 MB. Handling of words missing from the pronunciation dictionary (names) so nothing is skipped. Licence notices in About | |
| **3a** | `phase-3-alarm-conversation` | **The alarm conversation.** Every ring's AlarmKit sound is the butler himself (recorded on the iPhone with the chosen sound under it): the time, which alarm this is, the next event or most important reminder, and "dismiss, or remind you again in N minutes? Press Respond". **Respond** opens Concierge Clock, which silences the ring, says it again on the loudspeaker and **listens** (speech recognised on the iPhone). The reply is understood by built-in rules in all 11 languages ("dismiss", "I'm up", "remind me in 10 minutes", "half an hour", "at quarter past seven", "a las 7") and by Gemma for anything else. He then does it — dismisses (one wake-log entry for the morning, scored with its snoozes) or schedules a new ring at that time — and confirms aloud. No answer twice, or not understood three times: he reminds again after the snooze length. Big Dismiss / Remind / Repeat buttons. If the app is already open when the alarm rings, the conversation starts by itself. Database version 4 (`alarmOccurrence.originID`) | **Built, awaiting device test** |
| 3 | `phase-3-voice-nightstand` | (rest of Phase 3) **Alarm sessions** (wake-up) and **reminder sessions** (events and reminders) behave separately. *Alarm session:* **every time an alarm sounds the butler asks whether to dismiss or remind again** and the reply is understood with on-device speech recognition plus Gemma (rules when Gemma is not available); Nightstand Mode, the wake briefing on the loudspeaker, listening for snooze ("give me 10 minutes", default 5) and dismiss ("I'm awake"), "Are you really up?", snooze escalation and limit, bedtime preview, sunrise screen, pre-recorded briefing as the AlarmKit sound. *Reminder session (per item):* each event or reminder is read out, then the butler **pauses and listens**; replies such as "OK, I understand", "Yes, I'll go", "Dismiss", "Remind me in 5 minutes", "Remind me at 3", "Set an alarm for 6", "Mark it done", "Repeat" or anything similar are understood by the **offline AI (Gemma)**, which decides the next step: no further reminder, a **future reminder** (AlarmKit ring labelled with the item) or a **new alarm**, and the butler confirms it aloud. A rule-based parser handles the common phrases and every iPhone without Gemma. No answer = remind again at the next step (before the start, then at the start). Speech recognised on the iPhone; barge-in with echo cancellation; Speaking / Listening / Done indicator with big buttons | |
| 4 | `phase-4-sounds` | Ambient sound for Nightstand Mode: synthesised white / pink / brown noise (from FocusFound), rain, soft pad, sleep timer, separate volumes, gradual ramp-up | |
| 5 | `phase-5-trends` | Scheduled vs. actual charts (7 / 14 / 30 days / all time), wake score, weekday vs. weekend, Wake Optimization Advisor with one-tap suggestions and 7-night experiments, Apple Health sleep (opt-in) | |
| 6 | `phase-6-offline-ai` | Gemma, further: "Ask me about my day" (questions and answers), varied lines in every tone, a short written summary per event, weekly wake summary (importance ranking arrived in Phase 2, conversation understanding in Phase 3) | |
| 8 | `phase-8-siri-polish` | Siri and Shortcuts (App Intents: brief me, start a reminder session, wake trends, skip tomorrow), StandBy clock face, accessibility pass, UI tests, device × iOS test matrix | |
